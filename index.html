    Classes

    Adding your schedule to Google Calendar
    1. Choose Export → iCalendar (.ics) to download your calendar file.
    2. In Google Calendar, create a new calendar for the term first. Deleting that calendar later removes every class at once.
    3. Go to Settings → Import & export → Import, choose the .ics file, pick the new calendar, and click Import.
    4. The .ics keeps classes as weekly repeating events with holidays skipped. The CSV lists every meeting as its own event, which is useful for spreadsheets or apps that don't read .ics.

    Changes show in the preview right away and apply to every export.
